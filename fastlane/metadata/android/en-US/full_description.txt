One disc, one needle, and your finger on it. Runout turns a piece of audio into
something you play with your hand rather than something you press play on.

There is no progress bar. Position on the disc is position in the sound,
continuously, so scrubbing feels like it is on the material rather than commanding
a player that responds a moment later.

WHAT IT IS
• A single record you can scrub, hold, nudge and release.
• Groove banding that shows the shape of the music, so you can see where the loud
  part is before you get there.
• Drop in any audio file from your device and play it this way.

WHAT IT IS NOT
It is not a music player, a library or a streaming client. It has no playlists and
no catalogue. It is an instrument for one piece of audio at a time.

BUILT AROUND LATENCY
The whole point is the feel, so the audio path is built for it: the scrub target is
under thirty milliseconds of perceived delay. If that number is not met the thing
is not worth shipping, which is why it is the first thing the project measured.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your audio files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Runout ships with a synthesised test pressing so there is something to play
immediately. No copyrighted audio is bundled.

This is an early version, focused entirely on whether the feel is right.
