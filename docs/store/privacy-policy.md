# Privacy Policy — Runout

> **This is the working copy, not the hosted one.** The URL Play Console points at
> is **https://asystemofcells.com/runout/privacy**. Keep the two in sync by hand.

**Last updated: 29 August 2026**

Runout is an audio instrument for Android (package `com.runout.app`), made by
A System of Cells. This policy is short because the app does almost nothing with
data.

## The short version

Runout has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. **Nothing
leaves your device.**

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** We operate no servers and receive nothing.

## Your audio files

When you drop in an audio file, the app reads it from your device and plays it.
That file is never uploaded, never copied anywhere off the device, and never
analysed for any purpose except drawing the groove and playing the sound.

The app ships with a synthesised test pressing so there is something to play
immediately. No copyrighted audio is bundled.

## What is stored, and where

Your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling
Runout deletes them. Audio files you opened stay where they were; they were never
the app's to move.

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to an audio file you pick | Only the file you choose, through Android's own file picker, only while you are playing it. |

The app requests no other permission. If you are reading a permission list that
shows more, that is a packaging mistake rather than a feature, and it should be
reported.

## Children

Runout is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it, and the
